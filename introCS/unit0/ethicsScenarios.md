# Scenario 1

Mr. Baier is attending a concert this weekend. After school one day, he prints the tickets at Hun using his school laptop and printer. Is this ethical?

# Scenario 2

Mr. Baier develops a program for Hun teachers to use to create their Raidernet homepages as part of his job at Hun. He shares that program with a friend who works at Lawrenceville. How would you judge Mr. Baier’s behavior?

# Scenario 3

A Hun student finds that a teacher has left their laptop open in their office unattended. There is an email open on the screen addressed to the Upper School Faculty only. The student reads the email but doesn’t do anything with the information learned. The student rationalizes that they only read it out of curiosity. How would you rate this student’s behavior?

# Scenario 4

There is a paper due for Computer Science at the end of the day on Friday. Mr. Baier has told the class that he will be absent that day and will not look at the papers until Monday, but that any paper marked as Late by Raidernet will lose points. A student submits a blank Google doc on Friday so that Raidernet will not count their submission as late, then completes the assignment over the weekend. How would you rate this student’s actions?

# Scenario 5

Student A is enrolled in an art class where they are using a fun computer drawing program. Each student needs an account to access the program and the school pays for the students in the class. Student B loves art and wants to use the program but is not enrolled in the class. Student A gives Student B their username and password so that they can log in and use the program for free.  How would you judge Student A’s behavior?

# Scenario 7

A school publishes a list to display on their webpage reporting all the colleges and universities where their senior class received acceptance letters. A college counselor is asked by the head of school to include several schools on that list even though no students were accepted there this year. When the counselor questions this request, they are told that students in past years were accepted at that school, so it doesn’t matter. The counselor is worried about losing their job if they ignore the request so they comply. How would you rate the counselor’s actions?

# Scenario 8

Morgan disagrees with a classmate in an online forum. Frustrated, she posts a sarcastic and critical comment, tagging the classmate by name. How would you rate Morgan’s actions?
