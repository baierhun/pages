# Machine Learning Experiment

## Assignment

Complete the three parts of the **Machine Learning Experiment**.

* **Part 1:** Complete with your partner — **one sheet per pair**
* **Part 2:** Complete with your partner — **one sheet per pair**
* **Part 3:** Complete individually — **one sheet per student**

### What to Submit

**On paper:**

* ☐ Part 1 — one per pair
* ☐ Part 2 — one per pair
* ☐ Part 3 — one per student

**On Canvas:**

* ☐ Your group's **Teachable Machine `.tm` file**
* Each student must submit a copy of the `.tm` file.

### Downloading Your `.tm` File

When your model is finished in Teachable Machine:

1. Click **File**.
2. Select **Save project** → **Save project to your computer**.
3. Your project will download as a **`.tm` file**.
4. Upload that file to the Canvas assignment.

**Do not submit a link to your Teachable Machine project. Submit the `.tm` file.**
