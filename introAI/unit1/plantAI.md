### Guided Listening Questions

**Directions:** As you listen, fill in the answers to each question in order.

---

### **Part 1: The Plant and Pipe Mystery**

1. Who are Alvin and Larry Ubell, and what surprising thing do they notice about tree roots and water pipes?
<br>
<br>

2. What hypothesis does Monica Gagliano test in her first experiment with plant roots in a Y‑shaped pot?
<br>
<br>

3. In the first experiment, how do the plants behave in the Y‑shaped pot when exposed to a real water pipe (outside the soil)?
<br>
<br>

4. What did Gagliano test next using **sound** instead of real water, and how did the plant roots respond?
<br>
<br>

---

### **Part 2: Mimosa Learning Experiment**

5. What is special about the *mimosa pudica* plant introduced in the second part of the episode?
<br>
<br>

6. In Gagliano’s second experiment, what happens to mimosa plants when they are dropped repeatedly? How does their response change?
<br>
<br>

7. How long did the mimosa plants *remember* the experience of being dropped, according to Gagliano’s experiment?
<br>
<br>

---

### **Part 3: Plant Memory and Learning Over Time**

8. In Monica Gagliano’s experiment, what did the blue light represent, and why did the plants grow toward it?
<br>
<br>

9. What was the purpose of using a small fan in the experiment?
What made it a good “neutral stimulus” (like Pavlov’s bell)?
<br>
<br>

10. After the training period (fan + light together), what happened when the plants were exposed to only the fan?
Why was this result important?
