# 🎬 AI Creative Studio Project

## Overview

In this project, you will use AI as a **tool** (not a shortcut) to create something interesting to *you*. This is an open-ended creative challenge: you choose the topic, you choose the format, and you use AI to help bring it to life.

There is no required format list — it's up to you. Some possibilities: a comic, a short film, a game, a music video, an audio drama, a website, an animated short, a piece of interactive fiction, a podcast episode, or something else entirely. If you're not sure your idea fits, just check with me.

---

## 🧠 Project Goal

Learn how to:

* Generate ideas with AI and build on them
* Improve weak AI-generated content instead of accepting the first result
* Combine **multiple types of AI** into one finished creative product
* Manage a multi-day creative project and show your process

---

## 📅 Suggested Timeline

This project will take several class periods — plan on it being a real time investment, not something to finish in one sitting. Use this as a general guide and adjust based on your project:

### **Early – Concept + Planning**

* Brainstorm your idea/topic
* Decide on a format
* Decide which types of AI you'll use and why
* Start experimenting with prompts

### **Middle – Building**

* Generate and revise content
* Start assembling your project
* Keep your working process visible (see Rules below)
* Improve or replace weak AI outputs as you go

### **Later – Revising + Combining**

* Continue building and refining
* Combine pieces into a cohesive whole
* Start thinking about how you'll present it

### **Final – Finishing + Presenting**

* Final polish
* Prepare your presentation
* Present your project to the class

---

## 💡 Need Ideas?

You don't have to have it all figured out yet. A good project usually starts with a small, specific idea rather than a big vague one. For example, instead of "I want to make a comic," try something like "I want to make a comic about a superhero whose power is also their biggest weakness." Instead of "I want to make a music video," try "I want to make a music video about a memory that still bothers me."

A few directions that tend to work well, just to get you thinking — a visual story told with little or no dialogue, a song built around a feeling or place that matters to you, an interactive experience where the "goal" is really a metaphor for something else, a scene-based audio piece set entirely in one small space, or a mockumentary-style piece about something absurd you invent. These are just starting points, not a menu — your final idea can look nothing like these.

---

## 🔒 Important Rules

* You **must use at least two different types of AI** (for example: text/writing AI **and** image AI, or music AI **and** voice AI). Using two tools that do the same thing (like two text generators) does not count.
* You **must use AI more than once per tool** — not just a single prompt. Show real back-and-forth revision.
* You **must improve on weak AI output** — don't just accept the first result AI gives you.
* This project requires a genuine time commitment across multiple work sessions. Projects that look rushed or last-minute will not meet the requirements, regardless of final quality.

---

## 🧩 Final Submission

You will turn in:

### 1. Final Project

Whatever you created — comic, game, video, film, audio piece, or something else

### 2. Proof of Process

You must show evidence that you spent real time and effort on this project. This can include (choose what fits your project):

* Version history from Google Docs, Google Slides, or similar (showing edits over time)
* A running log of every AI prompt you used, in order, including revisions
* Screenshots/exports of AI conversations or generations at different stages
* Drafts or early versions of your work compared to the final version
* A short written reflection describing your process day by day

### 3. Presentation

You will present your finished project to the class, including:

* What you made and why
* Which types of AI you used and for what
* One thing that didn't work well and how you fixed or worked around it

---

## 💡 Final Reminder

AI can help you start — but it's your job to **improve, adapt, and create something meaningful**.

If you rely on a single prompt per tool, or rush the process, your project will not meet the requirements.
