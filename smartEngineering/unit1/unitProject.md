# Unit 1 Final Project: Smart Appliance Prototype

## Overview

For your in-class final project, you will build a **control panel for a household appliance** using an Arduino and physical components.

You are building the appliance’s **interface**—the controls a person uses and the indicators that show how the appliance responds.

## What You Will Build

Your prototype must:

- Represent a household appliance.
- Use **3 different types of physical inputs**.
- Use **3 physical outputs**.
- Give each input and output a clear purpose.
- Connect inputs and outputs in ways that make sense for your appliance.
- Use correct breadboard wiring.

You will receive the full building instructions and component requirements in class.

## Individual Demonstration

When your prototype is complete, you will have a brief individual conference with me. You will:

- Demonstrate your working prototype.
- Explain what each input and output does.
- Explain how your circuit is connected.
- Answer questions about Unit 1 electronics concepts.

## How to Prepare

Review inputs and outputs, breadboard wiring, LEDs and resistors, potentiometers, voltage, current, resistance, Ohm’s Law, and series and parallel circuits.

Be prepared to **explain these ideas in your own words and apply them to a circuit**.
