# Unit 1 Final Project

## Project Format

You will design and build a **physical device interface**.

Your project will use:

**3 physical inputs**

and

**3 physical outputs**

The inputs and outputs should work together to create a **useful and believable device**.

**You may not use a computer for this project, but you can use notes**

---

## Required Components

### Inputs — Choose 3

You must use **three different inputs** from the components we have learned.

Possible inputs:

* Push button
* Potentiometer
* Slide switch

### Outputs — Choose 3

You must use **three outputs** from the components we have learned.

Possible outputs:

* LED
* Active buzzer
* Multiple LEDs

You may use multiple LEDs as separate outputs if they represent **different indicators or functions**.

---

## Your Design

Your device should have a clear purpose.

Each input should have a specific job.

Each output should have a specific job.

Your inputs and outputs should **interact in a meaningful way**.

For example:

> Turning a potentiometer changes a setting.

> Pressing a button starts an action.

> Moving a switch changes a mode.

> An LED shows the current status.

> A buzzer indicates that an action is complete.

---

## Project Requirements

Your prototype must:

1. Have a clear purpose.
2. Include **3 different physical inputs**.
3. Include **3 physical outputs**.
4. Give each input a clear purpose.
5. Give each output a clear purpose.
6. Have the inputs and outputs interact in a meaningful way.
7. Use the components safely and correctly.
8. Be built on a breadboard.
9. Be able to be demonstrated to the teacher.

---

# Individual Project Check

When your prototype is complete, you will demonstrate it **individually** with the teacher.

You should be able to explain:

### Your Device

* What is your device?
* What does it do?

### Your Inputs

* What are your three inputs?
* What does each input do?
* Why did you choose each input?

### Your Outputs

* What are your three outputs?
* What does each output indicate?
* How does each output respond to an input?

### Your Circuit

* Where is **5V** connected?
* Where is **GND** connected?
* Why does an LED need a resistor?
* How are your components connected?

---

# Unit 1 Knowledge Check

During your individual discussion, you will also answer questions about concepts from Unit 1.

You may be asked about:

* **Voltage**
* **Current**
* **Resistance**
* **Ohm's Law**
* **V = IR**
* Series circuits
* Parallel circuits
* How voltage behaves in series and parallel circuits
* How current behaves in series and parallel circuits
* Why resistors are used with LEDs
* What happens when resistance increases or decreases
* What happens when voltage increases or decreases
* Potentiometers
* Inputs and outputs

You should be able to **explain these ideas in your own words**, not just give a definition.

---

# Project Conference

Your individual conference will be approximately **5 minutes**.

You will:

1. Show your working prototype.
2. Demonstrate all 3 inputs.
3. Demonstrate all 3 outputs.
4. Explain how your inputs and outputs work together.
5. Answer several Unit 1 electronics questions.

Your prototype does not need to be complicated.

It needs to demonstrate that you understand how **physical inputs and outputs can be combined to create a useful device interface**.
