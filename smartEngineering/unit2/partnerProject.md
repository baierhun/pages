You and **one partner** will design and build a smart device using an Arduino.

We will work together as a class to assign each group a **different project**.

## Project Options

### 🚗 Smart Parking Sensor

Use an ultrasonic sensor to detect how close a car is. Use three outputs to provide increasingly urgent warnings as the car gets closer.

### 🌡️ Temperature Alert

Use a TMP36 to measure temperature. Use three outputs to indicate whether the temperature is cold, comfortable, or hot.

### 🔐 Security Alarm

Use a button and/or ultrasonic sensor to detect an intrusion. Use three outputs to provide visual and/or audio alarm signals.

### 🎮 Reaction Game

Create a game that uses buttons as inputs and LEDs and/or a buzzer as outputs. Players must react to changing signals as quickly as possible.

### 🚶 Crosswalk Signal

Use a button to request a crossing. Use LEDs and a buzzer to simulate a pedestrian crossing signal.

### 🚨 Emergency Warning System

Use buttons and/or sensors to detect an emergency condition. Use three outputs to provide different warning signals.

## Requirements

Your project must:

* Be completed with **one partner**.
* Use an **Arduino**.
* Have at least **2 inputs**.
* Have at least **3 outputs**.
* Use **digital input/output**.
* Use **analog input and/or output**.
* Respond to changing input values.
* Have a functioning circuit and program.
* Be something that **both partners can explain**.

## Demonstration

You and your partner will demonstrate your project and explain:

1. What your device does
2. What your **inputs** are
3. What your **outputs** are
4. How the inputs control the outputs
5. How your Arduino program works
