# Heart of Hun — Project Preparation

1. **Finish your project.** Make sure your code and circuit are working correctly. Test all inputs and outputs.

2. **Prepare your inputs for display.** Use your partner’s breadboard and jumper wires to connect your inputs to your partner’s bread board. This will allow visitors to interact with your project when it is displayed.

3. **Test the connections.** Make sure your inputs work correctly when connected to the other board and that your project still functions as intended.

4. **Get ready to display.** Organize your wiring, secure your components, and make sure everything is neat and presentable.

5. **Explain your project.** Write a short, succinct instruction sheet for your project. Include:
    * A description of your project
    * What are the inputs and how does someone use them
    * What are the outputs and what do they represent

5. **Final check.** Test your project one last time and make sure it is ready for people to interact with at Heart of Hun.

6. **Ready to go?** If your project is finished, tested, and ready to display, you may bring it up for display with the instruction sheet!
